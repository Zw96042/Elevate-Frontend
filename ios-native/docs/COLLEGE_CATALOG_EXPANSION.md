# College catalog expansion

Verified against primary university publications on **October 4, 2026**. This file documents the 62 added profiles in `CollegeProfileCatalogExtra.swift`; the original 16 profiles are maintained separately.

## Reporting rules

- Class GPA facts describe their named population and publication date. Admitted, incoming-announcement and enrolled figures are kept distinct.
- A middle 50% is entered as the 25th–75th percentile range only when the source identifies it that way. Medians are not interpolated. Emory's unweighted range stays in prose because its current page does not explicitly identify GPA quartiles.
- A source saying “4.0 scale” does not establish unweighted reporting. `publishedGPAScale` is explicit only when the publication identifies weighting; values above 4.0 retain a warning rather than an assumed formula.
- No personal percentile, admissions odds, class rank or admission guarantee is inferred. Guidance-only rows contain school-specific academic review or preparation information, without fabricated class averages.
- GPA coverage is included only where explicitly published: Carnegie Mellon 99.7% and Auburn 99.95%. Other profiles leave coverage unknown.
- `.reported4Point` denotes an undisclosed or unsupported admissions calculation. It must not imply that a weighted publication matches a student's unweighted estimate. A formula can be assigned separately only after its subject, year, credit and weighting rules are verified.
- The expansion contains 22 numerical class GPA profiles (16 ranges and 7 means, with Georgia providing both), plus Emory's scoped published range in prose, and 39 preparation/review profiles. All 62 have an exact primary source URL.

## Integration and verification

Georgia's class facts use its [current academic-core definition](https://admissions.uga.edu/apply/first-year-applicants/first-year-criteria/): five core areas plus AP Art and Music Theory, AP/IB weighting without double-counting school-added grade weight, and no Honors or dual-enrollment bonus. Its [self-reported grade instructions](https://admissions.uga.edu/admissions/first-year/self-reported-grades/) cover grades 9–11 and completed grade-12 courses, use the high school's grading scale, and exclude in-progress or non-A–F marks. Florida's linked decision-process page supplies its core weighting definition; Florida State's definition is linked separately above. UGA, UF and FSU use their corresponding supported methods; UCF's core-weighting FAQ supplies a fourth core method during integration. Unverified course eligibility or low-grade bonus treatment withholds the estimate, as described in `COLLEGE_GPA.md`.

The expansion is a separate static array so the main catalog can append it without replacing the original sources. Search aliases include common abbreviations and campus names, while stable IDs remain distinct from the original 16. No new dependency, request endpoint, student-data upload or grade-policy change is involved.

Source limitations remain visible in each profile. In particular, UC San Diego, Irvine, Davis and Santa Barbara's campus ranges do not establish the honors cap from the linked page; they must not silently use UCLA's fully weighted calculation. NYU's preparation guidance is Tisch-specific, Columbia's guidance is explicitly from 2023–24, and school guidance does not substitute for requirements for an individual program.

Focused verification passed: Swift type-check with the current model; 78 unique combined IDs and names; 62/62 primary HTTPS source URLs and nonempty context; 16 finite ordered ranges, 7 finite means and 2 reporting coverage values within 0–100. App navigation, rendering and device behavior require integration verification by the main task.

Final source audit on October 4, 2026 confirmed all 22 numerical profiles against the cited primary publications, including their cohort, population and weighting labels. It corrected Georgia Tech's conditional recalculation guidance, TCU's enrolled population and Trinity's August data date. SMU's large brochure could not be opened by the page reader, but the primary PDF's indexed text directly confirmed its admitted-class scope, July 2025 date and unweighted middle-50% range. Emory's separate published range remains prose because GPA quartile endpoints are not explicitly identified.
